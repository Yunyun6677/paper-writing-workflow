# Chinese economics journal routing

Do not assume one generic “Chinese journal style.” Build a target profile from current author instructions and a full-text corpus from the chosen journal.

## Journal families

- General economics and theory-oriented journals.
- Applied microeconomics and industrial-economics journals.
- International trade, finance, agricultural/rural and development field journals.
- Management, public administration and interdisciplinary policy journals.

For each target, verify and record:

- official author-guideline URL and retrieval date;
- language, length, abstract/keyword and classification requirements;
- anonymization and separate title-page requirements;
- citation/bibliography style;
- table, figure, appendix and online-supplement rules;
- data/code disclosure and ethics requirements;
- submission file formats and system constraints.

Infer rhetorical preferences only from full texts actually read. Keep journal rules separate from learned writing patterns: formal requirements come from current official guidance; style observations come from a documented article corpus.

The writing plan should explain what changes across targets: theoretical framing, hypothesis use, institutional detail, identification exposition, policy discussion, contribution language and appendix allocation. Never reshape empirical facts to imitate a journal.
