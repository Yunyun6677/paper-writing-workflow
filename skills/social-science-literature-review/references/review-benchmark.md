# Influential review benchmark protocol

Use this protocol for a full literature review or when the user requests writing-method benchmarking.

## Selection

Select five to eight review articles, handbook chapters, field surveys, systematic reviews, or meta-analyses close to the research question. Use at least three journals or publication platforms and balance recognized classics with recent reviews.

For each benchmark, record:

- exact bibliographic identity and stable identifier;
- topical and review-type comparability;
- citation or influence indicator, database, retrieval date, and coverage bias;
- legal full-text source and inspection status;
- review type and method transparency.

Citation counts are time-, database-, field-, and age-dependent. Prefer field- or year-normalized indicators, authoritative field-journal surveys, handbook status, and convergent signals. Raw citation count must not determine quality or exceed 15% of a benchmark score. If influence cannot be verified, mark it `impact-unknown`; never estimate it.

## Learning target

Extract transferable decisions rather than wording:

- how the review narrows and problematizes the question;
- organizing principle: chronology, theory, mechanism, method, context, or controversy;
- how paragraphs move from study-level evidence to comparison and synthesis;
- how contradictions, null findings, and boundary conditions are handled;
- how tables and figures carry argumentative work;
- how evidence and method gaps become research questions;
- how the conclusion separates established knowledge, uncertainty, and agenda.

Record each feature with a page or section locator. Mark it `adopt`, `adapt`, or `reject`, with a project-specific reason. Do not imitate distinctive phrases, paragraph sequences, or a living author's voice.

Benchmark reviews guide rhetoric and architecture. They may support substantive claims only when they separately satisfy the normal evidence-card requirements. Do not treat their summaries of primary studies as primary evidence.

## Output and gate

Produce `review-style-benchmark.json` and `.md` with:

- reproducible selection table;
- structural commonalities supported by at least two benchmarks;
- conditional techniques appropriate only to certain review types;
- anti-patterns;
- a project-specific writing specification covering opening move, organizing spine, paragraph rhythm, disagreement treatment, visual plan, gap logic, and conclusion structure;
- a short borrowing statement explaining how structure was learned without copying content or wording.

The lead approves this specification before full drafting. A full review is incomplete if fewer than five benchmark full texts were inspected, unless the run report documents a field-specific scarcity and the user accepts the limitation.
